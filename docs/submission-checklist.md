# Submission checklist

- [x] Publish the clean source and synthetic data to `Jolinsjhp/NextPlay-AI-Synthetic`, without copying the old `.git` directory or data archive.
- [ ] Deploy this build; verify synthetic replay, a decision comparison and evidence references online.
- [ ] Configure the model server-side and record a successful real four-role call; mocks are not provider validation.
- [ ] Publish a subtitled English demonstration under two minutes on an accepted public video platform. Show the question, numerical choices, hypothetical rollout, role/tool trace and audit. Label accelerated waiting.
- [ ] Insert real public repository, deployment and demonstration URLs in the submission form.
- [ ] Check account credit, rate limits, alerts and availability through the judging period. Do not raise paid limits without the account owner's decision.
- [ ] Confirm source-code licensing and third-party dependency obligations; a clean data scan is not a legal eligibility certificate.

Official requirements: https://github.com/microsoft/insidethegamehackathon/blob/main/OFFICIAL%20RULES.md

The code/data handoff is separate from the external publication steps above.
